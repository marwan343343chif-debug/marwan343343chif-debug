Who I am
I'm a novelist and solo indie developer currently adapting my completed novel Eternal Memories into an anime-style open-world action RPG gacha game — ETM for short.
The game isn't a commercial bet. It's a love letter. Every line of code, every shader, every lore fragment exists because the story deserved to be more than words on a page.
English is my second language. The worlds I build are my first.

What I'm building
Eternal Memories (ETM) — a solo-developed gacha RPG adapted from my own novel.

Anime-style open-world action RPG
Cosmological lore spanning 10 named layers + a hidden 0th Layer + the Labyrinth
A protagonist carrying 13 years of silence and a soul sealed inside him by heavenly contract
Real-time VFX systems: dissolve shaders, dimensional rifts, sanity-erosion mechanics
A dual-world mirror mechanic — two realities rendered simultaneously through layer culling and RenderTextures
The NeverLand Director — a sanity system that reshapes the world as the player fractures

Any revenue goes back into the project. That's the whole plan.
